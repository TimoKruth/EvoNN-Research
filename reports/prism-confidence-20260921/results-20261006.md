**No variant beats every other variant on either primary panel. No automatic
promotion is justified.** None of the six v2 variants beats `open` under the
frozen 110-contrast Holm rule. The following numbers are direction-aware mean
symmetric effects across paired seeds, not accuracy percentage-point differences.
Intervals are descriptive 95% paired-bootstrap intervals, not simultaneous CIs.

`frontier_v2` is promising on regression and images, but does not improve every
language task. `broad` is a useful language control, including on delayed copy.
Averaging and regularization alone do not establish improvement over `open`.
This does **not** prove that those components are useless inside frontier: their
interactions require an ablation. Calibrated selection is exactly neutral on
breadth quality, as expected for a regression-only change. Frontier winner-fit
training times average 123.78 seconds on core256 and 286.50 on breadth128, versus
45.59 and 159.82 for open (all four tasks included). These are selected-fit times,
not total search runtime or equal-compute efficiency measurements.

Interpretation remains **within Prism, fixed fits, amended execution**. Multiple
historical producers and raised safety caps were necessary. Continuation records
retain 481 charged attempts and one invalid proposal from failed cells; earlier
qualification-validator failure costs remain in the original recovery record.
No protected-test, other-engine, equal-wall-time or universal superiority claim
follows from this study. The new combinations below were chosen after observing
these results and require fresh confirmation.

## Implemented, switchable hypotheses

`open` remains the default. All eleven historical controls retain their behavior.
The following candidates are fully executable through `--variant` or YAML:

| Variant | Classification / regression | Language modeling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `routed_v3` | The complete frontier_v2 policy | The complete broad policy |
| `lean_v3` | Frontier search, representations and calibrated regression selection; original unsmoothed loss, all-parameter decay, no averaging | Broad |
| `aligned_v3` | Lean, with classification checkpoint selection by accuracy and cross-entropy as the tie-breaker | Broad |

Routing depends only on declared task kind, never benchmark names, observed
validation scores or stored winning architectures. It changes both proposal and
training policy. In particular, the language branch retains broad's original
inheritance discounts and generation-zero epoch allocation; it is not broad
search accidentally paired with frontier training. Available families remain
unchanged for the chosen branch. Mixed packs resolve each task separately.
Training and optimizer backends remain independently switchable.

Accuracy-aligned selection addresses an implementation mismatch: the comparison
scores classification accuracy, while historical training selects minimum
cross-entropy. `aligned_v3` selects maximum validation accuracy, breaks exact
accuracy ties with minimum unsmoothed cross-entropy, and can retain a better
inherited epoch-zero checkpoint. Regression retains training-only calibration
and calibrated validation MSE. LM retains unsmoothed perplexity selection.
This changes checkpoint selection, not training-label loss or protected-test use.
It adds validation work and must earn its place in confirmation.

Resolved policies are recorded per task in telemetry and per attempt, checked by
the portable validator, and reconstructed from frozen configuration on resume.
The validator also checks the executed selection, smoothing, decay and averaging
settings for v3. Fixed architectures are accepted only when every task has the
required training policy; mixed LM v3 fixed-genome controls are rejected explicitly.
The existing deterministic two-million-parameter safety constraint is retained.

Run templates are under `EvoNN-Prism/configs/{routed,lean,aligned}_v3.yaml`.
Example:

```sh
.venv/bin/python -m prism.cli run --config EvoNN-Prism/configs/aligned_v3.yaml
```

For new comparisons, `python -m evonn_compare.prism_followup --base-spec
<all-engine-spec.json> --output <new-directory>` writes matched specs for open,
broad, frontier_v2 and all three v3 candidates. `--variants` selects candidates.
Every arm retains Prism, Topograph, Stratograph, Primordia and Contenders on every
base-spec cell. It rejects reused study seeds and the historical Prism-only scope;
the campaign schema also prevents applying that exception to v3. Original
confidence-study arms, inference and frozen producers remain untouched.

The generator prepares specifications only. No new long comparison was launched.
Before confirmation, freeze the candidate contrasts and task/sentinel regression
criteria, qualify resource allowances for the declared matrix, and use fresh
paired seeds. Compare quality together with total training work, model sizes,
failures and runtime. Nonsignificance is not an equivalence or no-regression result.
